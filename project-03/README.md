This portfolio is all about finding patterns in things from our daily lives, local weather patterns and pop music trends. 
With visualization, density modeling, and text mining, I want to find trends in data that people deal with every day but don't 
normally think about too hard. 

## Data and Analysis

### Part 1: Tampa International Airport (TPA) Weather Data (2022) (tpa_weather_2022.csv)

This project looks at daily climate stats from TPA throughout 2022, focusing on daily highs (°F) 
and total monthly rainfall (Inches).Tampa basically has two main seasons. Winter and spring 
are very random with temperature bounces from 45-85 and barely any rain. 
Summer on the other hand, locks into a super steady, super hot zone of 85-95 
from June through September, which brings tons of heavy rain and storms.

### Part 2: Billboard Top 100 Songs Analysis (BB_top100_2015.csv)

I analyzed tracking data for Billboard Hot 100 hits, looking at lyric lengths, word counts, 
and where they landed on the charts. A song's length doesn't really matter when it comes to hitting
number one, some tracks are packed with words while others are super sparse. Instead, the formula relies heavily 
on repetition and relatable, everyday words (lots of contractions like "im", "dont", and "wanna", and 
emotional staples like "love" and "baby").
